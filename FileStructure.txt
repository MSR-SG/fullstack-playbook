FULLSTACK PLAYBOOK: REPO STRUCTURE
==================================

Here is a structure that works as a practice ground and as a quick reference.
Think of it like a kitchen. The topic folders are your pantry, the cheatsheets
are your recipe cards, and the projects are the meals where everything comes together.

Every exercise folder should hold three things. A short README.md with the problem,
your notes, and the mistakes you made. A src/ folder with your code. A test/ folder
that proves the code works.

Two practical rules:

- Keep a separate build system per language. Use npm for JavaScript and React, one package-lock.json per app.
  Use one Maven or Gradle multi-module setup for Java and Spring.
  Use a virtual environment (.venv) and pytest for Python. If you force them
  into one build, you will waste time fixing tooling instead of practising.
- Never commit AWS credentials. Put them in .env, list .env in .gitignore, and check
  before every push.

One weak spot to watch: topic folders often turn into a graveyard of small snippets
you never open again. Your new job is full stack, so the real skill is joining the
layers: React calling a Spring API, which reads from RDS and deploys to AWS. Put most
of your effort into the projects/ folder, and use the topic folders only to drill the
weak parts you find there.
